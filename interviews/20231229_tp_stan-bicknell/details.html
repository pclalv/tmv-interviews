In this episode I have a candid catch up with the legend Thomas Pridgen. Thomas and I have talked about doing this for some time now so it was great to finally get on a call and dive into his story a little more. This is a relaxed conversation, where we discuss his early years as a player including his lessons with Tony Williams, his impact on the drumming community through the rise of Gospel Chops, his time with The Mars Volta, and being a father of 3 as an active touring musician. Thomas is currently playing for Residenté and Fever333, and can be found over on IG - Thanks for the time Thomas 👊🏻
